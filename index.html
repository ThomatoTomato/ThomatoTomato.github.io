Thomas Wilskow Thorbjørnsen

Hello! My name is Thomas, and I am a Ph.D. candidate at the University of Western Ontario (UWO), supervised by Professor Dan Christensen. I think about homotopy type theory, synthetic algebraic geometry, and constructive homological algebra.

My work is sometimes formalized in Rocq/Coq. Here, I mainly contribute to the Coq-HoTT library.

I completed my bachelor's and master's degree at the Norwegian University of Science and Technology in 2023, under the supervision of Professor Steffen Oppermann.
